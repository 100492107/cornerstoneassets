# Cornerstone AI Assets — Brand Guide

## Positioning

**Creative that feels made, not generated.**

Cornerstone AI Assets is the creative testing division of Cornerstone AI Enterprises. The visual identity should feel editorial, intelligent, premium and contemporary — closer to a specialist creative studio than a generic AI SaaS company.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #0B0E14 | Primary background, dark surfaces |
| Ink 2 | #121722 | Cards, panels, secondary surfaces |
| Paper | #F4F2EC | Primary text, light surfaces, CTA buttons |
| Muted | #9EA6B8 | Secondary text |
| Line | #2A3140 | Dividers, borders |
| Cobalt | #6B7CFF | Primary brand signal, links, highlights |
| Violet | #B18CFF | Secondary highlight, gradients |

Avoid using the Cornerstone AI Group gold palette. Assets must feel like a separate specialist creative business.

## Typography

**Display:** Cormorant Garamond — large headlines, editorial statements and occasional italic emphasis.

**UI / body:** Inter — navigation, paragraphs, buttons, forms, tables.

**Utility:** IBM Plex Mono — eyebrow labels, step numbers, technical metadata and compact labels.

## Layout language

- Editorial rather than dashboard-like.
- Large typography and intentional whitespace.
- Asymmetric compositions are preferred.
- Use dark full-bleed sections with occasional paper/ivory blocks.
- Avoid card-grid overload.
- Motion should be subtle and purposeful.
- Photography/visuals should feel art-directed rather than generic stock.

## Voice

Calm. Precise. Commercial. Creative without being theatrical.

Prefer:
- “One product. Six hypotheses.”
- “Creative that earns attention.”
- “AI is the production advantage. Human judgement is the standard.”

Avoid:
- “Revolutionary AI-powered solutions.”
- “10x your content.”
- “Game-changing.”
- Fake urgency or inflated promises.
